# CallCatch — Acquisition channel research (2026)

**Date:** 2026-09-14. **Scope:** channels for a $79–$149/mo SaaS (blended ARPU $107, 86% variable GM, per `FINANCIAL_MODEL.md`) sold to US HVAC / plumbing / electrical owner-operators with 2–15 techs. **Legend:** `[V]` verified against a vendor page or 2025–26 third-party source (URL + date seen in §7); `[V2]` secondary/aggregator source only; `[U]` unverified assumption. servicetitan.com, getjobber.com, housecallpro.com, acca.org, nexstarnetwork.com, necanet.org and phccweb.org were **blocked by the research proxy**; their details come from search-engine excerpts of those pages, tagged `[V2]`.

**Bottom line:** at $107 ARPU the affordable CAC ceiling is ~$300–$450 (3–4 months of gross profit at 4.5% churn). Four channels clear it inside 90 days: founder outbound (cash CAC ≈ $0), Meta lead ads + founder callback (CAC ≈ $300–$550), the in-product referral coupon ($214), and success-only affiliate/agency referral ($260–$385). Marketplaces (Jobber first) are the cheapest durable channel but need 2–4 months. Associations, distributors and best-practice groups are 6–12-month plays that need reference customers first.

## 4. Affiliate, referral and agency channels

- **In-product referral** (spec): one free month each side = $214 per referred customer, zero cash out; model assumes 3% of base/month from Dec `[U]`.
- **Affiliate program:** SaaS median recurring commission 22.5% of first-year revenue; 71% of SaaS programs pay recurring (20–30% for 12 months, 10–15% months 13–24); recurring programs generate 3.4× the partner-driven ARR of one-time bounties over 36 months `[V2]` PartnerStack 2026 via track360/wecantrack; B2B median 20% `[V2]` TrackRev. **Proposal:** 25% recurring for 12 months = $27/mo, $321 per retained customer, paid only on collected cash. Recruit HVAC/plumbing YouTubers, coaches, and owner Facebook groups (e.g. "Service Business Growth", 3K members `[V2]` servicefusion). Run on a Rewardful-style tool (~$49/mo `[U]`).
- **Agency / GoHighLevel white-label:** GHL plans $97 / $297 / $497 (SaaS Pro), unchanged 2025→26 `[V2]`; ~40,000 agencies, 1.2M+ businesses `[V2]` ghlexpertly; missed-call text-back is native and agencies commonly resell at $297/mo and mark up SMS/AI 2–3× `[V2]` ghlscaleup/apexure. GHL agencies are **competitors with a cost problem**: the client pays $297 for a generic workflow. Offer a **wholesale tier**: agencies buy Pro at $99 (33% off) and resell at $199–$297 with trade-specific AI they cannot build cheaply. CAC ≈ $50/mo margin, no cash. Start month 3 with 20 agencies from GHL Facebook groups and Upwork.
