# Sales playbook (founder-led, ~30 hrs/week)

Source: `PRODUCT_SPEC.md` §7. Volume and yield assumptions are `[U]`: ~250 dials/DMs per week → ~6% conversation → ~35% demo → ~45% close ≈ 3-7 customers/month. Plan: 2 in Sep, then 3, 5, 6, 7, 7, 7.

---

## 1. ICP

Owner-operator of a US residential **HVAC, plumbing or electrical** company, 2-15 techs, $500k-$5M revenue, where the owner or spouse still answers the business line from a truck. Already spends on Google LSA / Meta / Angi. Uses Jobber / Housecall Pro / ServiceTitan or paper. Misses 20-60 calls/month (industry 27-62% unanswered `[V2]`). Ticket $350 repair to $8-12k changeout. **Sun Belt first**: TX, FL, AZ, GA, NC (AC through October, heating tune-ups from October; plumbing/electrical year-round). Month 3+: garage door, pest, roofing.

Disqualify fast: call centers / answering service under contract with a termination fee they won't eat; > 20 techs (they have a CSR); no business line at all; anyone who wants us to text cold lists.

## 4. Scripts

### 4.1 Phone opener (after an unanswered call to their ad number)
> "Hey, is this Mike? Mike, I'm *[name]* with CallCatch. I called your number from your Facebook ad at 6:40 last night and got voicemail — which is normal, you were probably on a job. Quick question: when that happens, does the homeowner get anything back from you? … We text them back in ten seconds, ask what's wrong and where, and send you the lead. Ten-minute setup, you keep your number. Got 15 minutes tomorrow at 7:30am or noon so I can show you on your own line?"

If they say "text me the info": send the DM below immediately and a calendar link; call again in 48 h.

### 4.2 Maps-sourced opener (no ad)
> "Mike? *[name]* with CallCatch — I read your reviews, people keep saying you personally come out, which usually means you're the one answering the phone from the truck. When a call comes in while you're under a house, what happens to it? … "

### 4.3 DM / email (Ad Library or Maps sourced)
> **Subject:** called your ad number at 6:40pm
>
> Mike — called (555) 123-4567 from your Facebook ad last night, got voicemail. Not a knock; it's the normal outcome for owner-operators. We built CallCatch so those callers get a text back in 10 seconds, get asked what's wrong and where, and land on your phone as a booking-ready lead. No new number, 10-minute setup, $79/mo, 30-day money-back. Want me to run a free missed-call audit on your line this week? Takes 5 minutes. — *[name]*, founder

Follow-ups: day 2 "Here's the 30-second version: *[Loom]*"; day 5 "Last one — call *[demo number]* and don't answer when we call back. That's the whole product."; then archive for 60 days.

### 4.4 Group post (value only)
> Quick math I keep seeing with HVAC owners: 10 missed calls a week × ~30% you'd have closed × $450 ticket ≈ $1,350/week that went to whoever answered. Industry studies put contractor missed-call rates at 27-62%. If you want to know your real number, reply or DM me your business line and I'll pull a 5-minute missed-call audit for free (I call it after hours and log what happens). No pitch in the audit.

### 4.5 Voicemail (leave one, 15 seconds)
> "Mike, *[name]* with CallCatch. I called your ad number last night and it went to voicemail — I'm calling about what happens to those callers. I'll text you the short version. *[number]*."

## 5. The 12-minute demo

Screen share + their phone. Book at 7:30am or noon/3pm.

| Min | Step | Say / do |
|---|---|---|
| 0-2 | Diagnose | "Calls per week? Who answers? After 5pm? Average ticket?" Type the math on screen: missed/week × 30% × ticket × 4 = $/month. |
| 2-3 | **Call their line, let it ring** | "Forward your missed calls to this number for 5 minutes — Verizon `*71`, AT&T `*61*`, T-Mobile `**61*` — or just don't answer this call." Dial their business line from your phone; let it ring out. |
| 3-6 | **Show the text** | Their phone (or yours, on camera) buzzes with the text-back within 10 s. Reply as a homeowner: "no cool air, 78704, today". Show the AI asking the next question, the lead card filling in, the owner alert on the other phone. |
| 6-8 | Inbox + takeover | Open `/inbox`, reply as the business, AI pauses. Mark Booked → est. revenue. |
| 8-9 | Weekly report | Show the Monday email from the demo account: missed / texted / booked / $ recovered. |
| 9-10 | Forwarding page | Their carrier's code, "Test my forwarding" button, quiet hours, alert phone. "Texting starts the day carriers verify your number — usually about a week; alerts and voicemail transcripts start today." |
| 10-12 | Close | §7. If yes: open `/signup` together and finish onboarding on the call (day-of checklist §9). |

Never demo without the demo account seeded and your own phone forwarded — if their carrier code fails, dial the demo line instead and show the same thread.

## 7. Closes

### 7.1 Close to annual
> "Two ways: $79 a month plus $149 for me to set it all up for you, or $790 for the year, setup included, two months free, 30-day money-back either way. Most owners take the annual because it's one less bill to think about. Which one?"

If they hesitate on annual → monthly + DFY ($79 + $149). **Never discount the monthly.** Pro: "$149 a month or $1,490 a year" with the same structure.

### 7.2 Pay-now with 30-day money-back
> "Let's do it now while we're on the call — I'll send the Stripe link, you're charged today, and if in 30 days it hasn't paid for itself you tell me and I refund it, no questions. Your monthly billing anchor moves to the day your number verifies, so you're not paying for verification days."

Send `https://callcatch.co/signup?plan=<starter|pro>&interval=<month|year>&path=paynow`. Stay on the call until the Stripe success page shows.

### 7.3 Trial (only if pay-now fails twice)
> "Then take the 14-day trial: card on file, the clock starts the day carriers verify your number, and you'll already have alerts and voicemail transcripts before that."

`…&path=trial`.

### 7.4 Upsell to Pro (on the call or at the trigger)
Triggers: they run Meta/web lead forms, want a second line, after-hours emergency routing, or a booking link. "Pro adds your web and Facebook leads into the same inbox with an instant text, after-hours routing to your on-call guy, and a booking link — $149."

## 8. Referral ask

On the day their **first job books** (you'll see it in `/admin` → milestones), call or text:
> "Saw your first CallCatch booking came in — nice. Who's one other owner you know who answers the phone from the truck? I'll give you both a free month when they sign up. Your link: callcatch.co/r/*[code]*"

Repeat in the weekly report email from month 2 (automatic). Track in `referrals`.

## 9. Day-of-onboarding checklist (founder, on the call)

- [ ] Step 1 legal name exactly as EIN letter; EIN entered (or sole-prop path).
- [ ] Number provisioned; greeting tone chosen; profile: services, never-say ("no firm prices"), price ranges optional, booking link.

- [ ] Forwarding code dialed on their phone, **Test my forwarding** green.
